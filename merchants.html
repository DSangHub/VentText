How it works for you

You stay in control

VentText only acts within limits you set — nothing gets auto-approved beyond what you're comfortable with.

1

Set your coupon ceiling

Decide the max value VentText can auto-issue without you — say, $10 or $15 per complaint.

2

Set your response window

Anything above your ceiling routes to you directly, with a deadline you choose — typically 24–48 hours.

3

Review it all in one place

Every complaint, resolution, and coupon issued shows up in a simple dashboard — no spreadsheets, no guessing.

Built-in protection

Real money is on the line, so the system is built to protect you from abuse on both ends.
